Meat Bazar is a comprehensive end-to-end digital marketplace and supply-chain management platform purpose-built for Bangladesh’s meat industry. It seamlessly connects farmers and collectors, distributors, and end customers on a single transparent ecosystem—eliminating exploitative middlemen, ensuring fair pricing, and guaranteeing reliable delivery of fresh meat products.
The platform operates as a full-stack solution that combines:

E-commerce – Customers can browse, order, and pay for meat products online via web or mobile apps.
Inventory & Stock Management – Collectors and admins maintain real-time stock levels with automatic low-stock alerts.
Order & Logistics Coordination – Distributors track, process, and fulfill orders with live status updates.
Secure Digital Payments – Integrated support for bKash, Nagad, and card payments, complete with digital receipts and transaction history.
Role-Based Analytics – Dedicated dashboards and reports for admins (sales), distributors (delivery performance), and collectors (income summaries).

Designed with local realities in mind, Meat Bazar supports both English and Bengali, complies with Bangladeshi e-commerce and food-safety regulations, and runs on modern cloud infrastructure with strong security (OTP verification, encrypted passwords, two-factor authentication, and role-based access control).
